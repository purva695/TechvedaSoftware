Overview

Blockchain Development shaped around your business

Blockchain is not the right answer for every software problem. TechVeda begins by examining the trust model, participants, data and business rules involved. If a distributed ledger offers a meaningful benefit, we help define an appropriate architecture and build the application components around it.

Organisations exploring blockchain need to consider more than writing a smart contract. Security, transaction costs, user experience, network choice, data privacy, upgrade strategy and operational responsibilities all affect whether a solution is practical.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about our services, process and how we work with you.

What services does TechVeda provide?

We offer app development, web development, AI solutions, blockchain development, UI/UX design, digital marketing, virtual assistant support and creative design.

Can you help if I only have an idea?

Yes. Start by explaining the problem, the people who will use the solution and the outcome you want. We help clarify priorities and plan next steps before development begins.

How long does a custom app or website take?

It depends on scope, features, integrations and platforms. After understanding your needs, we share a clear scope and timeline estimate.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. We offer maintenance, technical improvements and new features after launch. The level of support is agreed in your project plan.

Can you integrate AI into my existing processes?

Often, yes. We assess your current workflows and systems, then identify where automation or AI can help — and where a simpler solution is better.

How do we get started?

Send us your requirements through the contact form. We will review them, set up a discovery call and suggest a practical way forward.
